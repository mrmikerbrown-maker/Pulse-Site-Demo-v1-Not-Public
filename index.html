Fed scenario planner

End-of-day planning tool. Enter the cut and hike odds priced in and see how each Fed scenario could play out across assets, sized to current volatility and calibrated on recent history.

Read this first. Sensitivities come from about three years of daily data; FOMC-day estimates rest on only 24 events, so treat them as rough. Scenario sizes are assumptions. FX vols are realised, not implied. A planning aid, not a forecast or trading advice.

Typical move = vol ÷ √252 × event multiplier. Each scenario moves the 2yr yield by a set number of typical moves; other assets follow using their measured sensitivity per 10bp of 2yr move. σ under each number compares the move with a typical event-day move. "Hit rate" is how often the asset moved the expected way on days when the 2yr moved 3bp or more; "±" is one standard error on the sensitivity.
