How it all fits together

  1. A data release comes out, such as inflation or jobs.
  2. Traders compare it with what they expected. The gap is the surprise.
  3. The surprise changes what they expect the central bank to do with interest rates.
  4. That ripples out: bond yields move first, then the dollar, gold and stocks.
  5. Often the move is partly done already, and other news mixes in, so real days are messy.
