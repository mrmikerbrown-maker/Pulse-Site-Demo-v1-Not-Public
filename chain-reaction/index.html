Markets don't move at random. One event sets off a chain. Pick a starting point and follow it.

    How to read this
    • The four markets: the S&P 500 (US stocks), the US 10-year Treasury yield, the US dollar and gold.
    • Yields and prices: a bond's yield moves the opposite way to its price. When bond prices fall, yields rise.
    • Safe havens: assets investors tend to buy when frightened: government bonds, the dollar, the yen, the Swiss franc and gold.
    • Other countries: the same chains work for other central banks and currencies. A hot UK inflation print, for example, tends to lift UK yields and the pound.
    • Expectations matter most: markets move on what is new compared with what traders already expected, not on the news itself.
