How the × number is worked out
  1. Collect a year of daily moves. For each market we take its daily change over the past 12 months: % for index futures, currencies and commodities, basis points for bond yields, points for VIX futures. Futures prices are back-adjusted so a switch to the next contract month doesn't count as a move.
  2. Measure the typical move. We work out the standard deviation of those daily changes. That is "a typical day" for that market. Updated each night.
  3. Compare today with it. Today's move ÷ the typical day = the × number.
  4. Longer periods. For 1 week, 1 month and YTD, the typical day is scaled up by the square root of the number of trading days in the period (5, about 21, and the days so far this year).

As a guide: about 2 days in 3 come in under 1×, about 1 day in 20 goes past 2×, and past 3× happens a handful of times a year.
