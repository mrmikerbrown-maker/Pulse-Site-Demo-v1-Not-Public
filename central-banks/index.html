How to read this

Rate is the bank's main policy rate. The Fed sets a range, so it shows both ends. The ECB figure is the deposit rate, the one markets watch.

Last decision is a hike, a cut or a hold, in basis points (1bp = 0.01%). Cuts are green and hikes red, because cheaper borrowing is good news for stocks and bonds. Decided, awaiting data means the bank has announced, but its new rate hasn't reached the free data yet.

Next meeting shows when the decision is announced, in London time. The Bank of Japan has no fixed time, so it shows a rough one (marked *).

Rates: FRED (Fed, ECB), the banks' own data feeds, and the Bank for International Settlements (BoJ, RBNZ). A new decision can take a day to a week to show. Meeting dates: each bank's published calendar.
